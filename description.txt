[b]On the first start, the mod offers to create the snow textures and flames in advance. This takes a few minutes, but it is optional: you can cancel at any time, and missing textures are then created in game when they are needed.[/b]

A visual overhaul for fire, snow, weather and shadows in RimWorld 1.7.

Rain, snow, blizzards, fog, auroras and lightning all get a new look. You can turn each feature on or off individually in the mod settings.

I tested the performance in a colony with 241 mods and found no significant difference to vanilla. The results are in the performance section further down.

Snow settles on trees, bushes, buildings and items, including those added by other mods. Snow textures are generated from each object's own texture, so no compatibility patches are needed. Eaves from other mods are the only exception. See the compatibility section below for details.

Fire follows the shape of whatever is burning. Trees burn down to their trunks, while flames cover the outlines of pawns and animals. You can choose between three flame styles or switch back to vanilla fire in the settings.

Furnaces, torches, candles and other buildings with an open fire get flames at fixed spots. A fire mask can shape these flames exactly, see the compatibility section below.

During the day, trees, buildings, items, pawns and animals cast shadows that move with the sun. At night, shadows are cast by light sources such as torches, lamps and campfires. No shadows are drawn indoors.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EvilEyeGuy/WeatherRIMagined-Mod/workshop/h5_freezing_water.png?v=3[/img]
[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EvilEyeGuy/WeatherRIMagined-Mod/workshop/zufrieren.gif?v=5[/img]
When temperatures drop below freezing, lakes and other bodies of water freeze from the shore. Pawns can walk on the ice or remove it with the "Break ice" command. If you use Medieval Overhaul, breaking ice gives you ice blocks.

You can turn this feature off in the settings.

[img]https://raw.githubusercontent.com/EvilEyeGuy/WeatherRIMagined-Mod/workshop/einstellungen.gif?v=3[/img]
You can turn individual features off to use their vanilla appearance instead. Here is an overview of the main settings:

[list]
[*][b]General:[/b] Set a size limit for the texture cache, generate snow textures in advance, show a status line while textures are prepared, and prepare the map during loading to avoid effects popping in afterwards.
[*][b]Fire:[/b] Choose a flame style or vanilla fire, preview the styles, set the animation length to 12, 24 or 48 frames, and choose which burning objects and pawns get the new flames.
[*][b]Snow:[/b] Choose where snow appears, including plants, buildings, furniture, items, eaves and the ground next to walls. You can also adjust the number of snow stages for trees and eaves and how often snow updates while falling or thawing.
[*][b]Weather:[/b] Enable or disable individual weather effects and adjust their density from 25 to 200 %. You can hide weather under roofs and, optionally, mountains. You can also turn off freezing water and remove existing ice.
[*][b]Shadows:[/b] Choose which plants, objects, items and pawns cast shadows, and whether torches cast shadows at night. You can also enable soft shadows and adjust the length of tree shadows.
[/list]

Snow and flames work with objects and plants from other mods without compatibility patches. Weather added by other mods, such as Vanilla Expanded, keeps its original appearance.

If you use Medieval Overhaul, its candles, candelabras and candle stands use the new flames.

Harmony is required.

[b]For modders:[/b] eaves, flames on buildings (with fire masks and small candle flames) and excluding objects from snow are explained here: [url=https://github.com/EvilEyeGuy/WeatherRIMagined-Mod/blob/workshop/MODDING.md]notes for modders[/url].

I will keep the mod updated and look into reported bugs and conflicts. If you run into a problem, please leave a comment or open a discussion with a log and a short description of what happened.
